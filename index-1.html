Dear Maasi ♡

Happy Birthday to you, Maasi! 💗

I don't think I can ever explain in a few words how special you are to me. Since my childhood, you have filled my world with so much love, happiness and surprises. I still remember how you used to gift me toys and make me feel like the luckiest little kid in the world.

But the best gift you have ever given me isn't a toy or a surprise — it's your love. You have always stood by me, helped me whenever I was in trouble, and made things feel easier just by being there.

Even now, when I'm growing up, you still see your little boy in me. And honestly, a part of me will always be that little kid who feels safe, loved and happy because he has a Maasi like you.

Thank you for loving me like a mother, for caring about me, and for being one of the most beautiful parts of my life. I hope I can make you proud and give you even a little of the happiness you have given me.

May your life be filled with health, peace, laughter, love and countless reasons to smile. You deserve all the beautiful things this world has to offer.

Love you endlessly, Maasi ♡
